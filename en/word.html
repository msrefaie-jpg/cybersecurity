01. The Program Interface and the "File" Tab

The Word interface consists of 8 components: the title bar, the Quick Access Toolbar, the File tab, the Ribbon, the ruler, the scroll bar, zoom, and the status bar.

🧰 Analogy: The Ribbon is like an organized toolbox: the tabs are its big drawers (Home, Insert...), the groups are the compartments inside each drawer (Font, Paragraph...), and the commands are the tools themselves. Most exam questions = "In which drawer and which compartment is the tool?"
🎀

The Ribbon Ribbon

Included in Office applications since Office 2007 — a direct exam fact. It consists of: tabs + groups + commands.

🗂️

The File Tab = Backstage

Clicking it displays the Backstage view: New, Open, Save, Print (Ctrl+P), Share, Export, Close (Ctrl+W). A new document is automatically named Document 1.

💾

Save Formats

Eight formats: docx (the default), doc (compatible with 97-2003), dotx/dot (templates), RTF, txt, htm, PDF — and never exe (the exception question).

📏

Status Bar & Ruler

The status bar shows: the page number, page count, word count, and language. The two rulers (horizontal and vertical) are shown from the Show group on the View tab.

03. Footnote or Endnote? And Which View Mode?

The two most confused points in the exam — settle them here:

⬇️

Footnote Footnote

Appears in the bottom margin of each page. Inserted from the References tab (not from Home!) — with the shortcut Ctrl+Alt+F.

🔚

Endnote Endnote

Appears at the end of the whole document — not at the bottom of each page. The official material states verbatim: "Footnotes appear in the bottom margin of each page, while the endnote appears at the end of the document."

🖨️

Print Layout Print Layout

The default view mode — and only in it do the header and footer settings appear. They don't show in Read Mode!

📃

Draft Draft

A mode for fast writing — headers and footers are not visible in it (per the official material). It is the answer to "Headers and footers were not visible in ... view."

💡 Memory trick: Foot = the foot of the page (bottom of every page) · End = the end of the whole book. And Track Changes: you can move between tracked changes and approve or reject them — the statement "you can't approve or reject" is always false.

06. What the Memo Doesn't Cover

The official material (114 pages, Word 2019) covers whole topics with no questions in the memo:

🎓

This section's place in the certificate

Word processing is program BIT 02 of the ten FDTC programs, lasting two days (two 120-minute sessions per day), and the course is built on Word 2019.

🔐

Document protection — 6 options

Read-only, encrypt with password (the highest level of security), mark as final, restrict editing, restrict access, and digital signature. The password is case-sensitive, its maximum length is 15 characters, and it cannot be recovered if lost.

✉️

Mail Merge Mail Merge

The "Mailings" tab, with five groups for creating bulk personalized letters and certificates. Recipient list sources: a new list, an Excel/Access file, or Outlook contacts.

📑

The four section breaks

Section Breaks: Next Page, Continuous, Even Page, Odd Page — they allow different orientations and headers within the same document.

🌀

Wrap Text Wrap Text

Seven options for how a picture relates to the text: In Line, Square, Tight, Through, Top and Bottom, Behind Text, In Front of Text — from the "Arrange" group on the Layout tab.

📇

Indexes and tables of authorities

Mark an index entry with Alt+Shift+X, the Table of Authorities (TOA) for legal citations, and citations in the APA / MLA styles for the social sciences.

🪞

Comparing documents

Compare on the Review tab: compares two files (original and revised) side by side with the differences highlighted — and merging them opens three windows.

🤖

Macros and Outline view

A macro records a series of clicks and typing to replay them with a button or shortcut. Outline view shows headings in levels from 1 to 9. And translation in Word requires an internet connection.
