Memo Explanations

A simplified explanation of everything covered in the memo's Presentations section (67 questions).

01. Presentation Basics

When you create a new blank presentation, it begins with a slide whose layout is the "Title Slide" — containing the main title and the subtitle. This is the first and most frequently examined fact.

🎬

Creating the Presentation and Inserting Slides

One way to launch it: press the Windows key, then choose PowerPoint. And there are five ways to insert a new slide (including Ctrl+M) — not just one!

🗂️

The Nine Layouts

Title Slide, Title and Content, Section Header, Two Content, Comparison (title + two content areas + two text placeholders), Title Only, Blank, Content with Caption, Picture with Caption.

📂

Organizing Slides into Sections

When slides are divided into sections, you can collapse, expand, rename or remove a section — verbatim wording from the material that matches the memo question.

🎯

Ordering Elements

The order of items within a slide changes by changing the order of the items themselves — not by reordering the slides (a famous trap). A single item can also carry more than one animation.

📏

Golden Design Rules

The 6×6 rule: no more than 6 lines per slide and 6 words per line. Title font size 48; body text no smaller than 24. Avoid too many pictures and avoid mixing drawn images with photographs.

🖼️

Slide Background

The slide background can be filled with a picture from your device or from the internet. Background formatting is on the Design tab, then "Apply to All" to spread it to every slide.

03. Saving & Extensions — Memorize Them Like a Times Table

Extension questions appeared in the memo four times in a row — the rule: an x at the end of the extension = modern versions; without it = a legacy version:

.pptx

Latest presentation format

The default and first in the list

.ppt

Legacy version

Compatible with 97-2003

.ppsx

Modern show file

PowerPoint Show — opens directly as a slideshow

.pps

Legacy show file

For older PowerPoint versions

.potx

Template

Saves layouts, colors and fonts for others to reuse

.pdf

Frozen format

"Formatting and layout freeze" — viewable without PowerPoint

💾 Also from the official material: you can save as an MP4 or wmv video, or as JPEG images (all slides or just the current one), and save to OneDrive "to access it from any device, anywhere". Printing comes in three color modes: Color / Grayscale / Pure Black and White — there is no "blue only"!

04. Animations & Transitions — The Essential Difference

A Transition = the visual effect that occurs when moving from one slide to another (verbatim definition from the material). An Animation, on the other hand, applies to items within a slide.

4️⃣

Four Animation Groups

Entrance, Emphasis, Exit, Motion Paths — four, not five (the memo's trap). Animations can also change an item's size or color.

⏱️

The Timing Group

Verbatim from the material: "In the Timing group you can control the duration of the transition and even set a sound to play during it" — not the "Sound" group!

1️⃣

One Transition… Multiple Animations

A single slide accepts only one transition, while a single item can carry more than one animation (e.g. entrance + emphasis + exit).

🎛️

Adding and Starting Animations

The command in the material is called "Add Animation" (the memo named it Add effects ⚠️). Start options: On Click / With Previous / After Previous.

06. What the Memo Doesn't Cover

The official material (145 pages, PowerPoint 2019) covers entire topics with no memo questions:

🎓

Program Origins and Its Place in the Certificate

Created by Robert Gaskins and Dennis Austin at Forethought and released on April 20, 1987, then acquired by Microsoft. This section is program BIT 03, lasting two days.

📊

The Three Chart Keys

When you insert a chart, three buttons appear beside it: Chart Elements (+) for titles and labels, Chart Styles for colors, and Chart Filters to show/hide data.

🔍

Slide Zoom

Three options: Summary Zoom (a full index), Section Zoom, Slide Zoom — giving your presentation a Prezi-like style.

🌊

The Morph Transition

Requires two slides sharing at least one common element — the easy way: duplicate the slide, then move the element.

🎯

Trigger and Animation Painter

A trigger links an item's animation to clicking another item (used in interactive quizzes). The Animation Painter, when double-clicked, works on multiple items and stops with Esc.

👑

The Slide Master

From the View tab: the master slide affects all layouts, while the layout masters are edited individually — to unify branding across all slides.

🔤

Proofing Underline Colors

Spelling errors get a red wavy line and grammar errors a blue wavy line — explicit wording in the material (Review tab).

🎥

Screen and Slide Recording

Screen recording is on the Insert tab: to select the entire screen press Win+Shift+F, and the minimum area is 64×64 pixels. Slide recording is on "Slide Show" with three views: Teleprompter, Presenter, Slide.

✔️

Symbols and Professional Touches

The check mark ✓ is Wingdings character 252, and the © symbol uses the shortcut Alt+Ctrl+C. Curved WordArt: Format → Text Effects → Transform.

📋

The Final Project

Individual or a team of 3 to 6 members: pick a topic, gather materials, build in PowerPoint 2019, add transitions and animations, put a logo on the slide master, save in multiple formats, and present to an audience.
