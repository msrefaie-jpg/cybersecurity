Memo Explanations

A simplified explanation of everything in the cloud computing section of the memo (50 questions).

01. Concept & Layers — Where Do Your Files Live?

Cloud computing gives you access to computing resources solely via the internet — your files and programs run on remote servers, not on your device.

⚡ Analogy: the cloud is like the power grid — you don't build a power station at home, you subscribe and pay for what you use. Likewise, instead of buying servers and software, you rent them over the internet and pay as you go.
🧩

Core Components

Cloud computing has 3 core components.

🎭

Front End Front End

Represents the users' layer — what you see and interact with.

🏗️

Back End Back End

Consists of 6 layers — the servers, storage, and management the user never sees.

🚀

Digital Transformation

Involves integrating digital technology into all aspects of business ✓ — and the provider famous for its extensive suite of tools to support it: Microsoft Azure.

02. The Three Service Models — Tap Each Model

The section's most common question: "Which model does company X provide?" — memorize the trio and its examples:

🍕 The pizza analogy: IaaS = they give you an equipped kitchen and you cook · PaaS = they give you the dough and the oven and you add the toppings · SaaS = a ready pizza delivered to your door. In all three: you never build the kitchen yourself!
✅

Cloud or Not?

Alibaba is a cloud service ✓ · while Cisco is not a cloud computing service (the "except" question!).

🧠

AI in the Cloud

PaaS contains ready-to-use software components for developing new capabilities, including innovative technologies: artificial intelligence, chatbots, and the Internet of Things (IoT).

03. The Microsoft World — OneDrive and Office Online

Numbers to memorize: 5 GB free, 93 days for the Recycle Bin, and Windows 10 — not 7:

💾

OneDrive — The Numbers

Free storage is 5 gigabytes · it comes pre-installed on Windows 10 (not 7!) · and with a work or school account, Recycle Bin items are deleted automatically after 93 days.

🔄

OneDrive — The Skills

Share files from File Explorer ✓ · restore an older version: right-click the file ← Version History · automatic backup of photos, videos, and screenshots from Settings ← Manage Backup (requires an internet connection and available storage space).

📽️

PowerPoint Online

Opens cloud presentations from any device via the browser after signing in to a Microsoft account · unified design with Master Slides is available in the desktop version only, not the cloud version ✓ · and printing has improved: full slides, with notes, or handouts of 3 slides per page (previously: only one slide per page).

🌀

Sway and Friends

Sway is Microsoft's service for creating online presentations and interactive reports ("prescription" is a spelling trap!) · Forms for polls and data collection · Quiz for electronic test forms · and Excel Online lacks the Data tab for importing data from databases.

04. The Google World — Drive, Photos, and Drawings

15 GB shared — not divided equally:

🗂️

Google Drive

Storage size is 15 gigabytes (not 25!) — and the space is shared between Gmail, Drive, and Photos, not divided equally between them (trap!).

📷

Google Photos

Video sharing ✓ · search for anything inside your photos — type bag to find photos of bags ✓ (searching your photos is done in Photos, not File Explorer!) · add an album: the Import button · and enabling backup saves photos and videos automatically to your account ✓.

👥

Google Contacts

Add an email address to contacts ✓ · export contacts in CSV format · and add a photo to a contact with the Add photo button.

✏️

Google Drawings

Drawings are created with Google Drawings (not Drive itself — trap!) · rename: click the "Untitled drawing" text area then press Enter ✓ (also from the File menu) · and a drawing can be downloaded as a PDF file ✓.

🎨

Canva in the Cloud

Canva is a cloud service in the field of design ✓ — and the Projects button opens the last file you worked on and the list of your stored projects.

📤

Broader Sharing

A Microsoft account unlocks more specialized programs like Sway for interactive reports and presentations ✓ — and a previously saved cloud presentation opens from any device after signing in.
